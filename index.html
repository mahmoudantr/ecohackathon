Why EcoHack exists

Sustainability challenges are real—and they need builders. EcoHack is designed as an annual sprint where teams ideate, build, and demo in a high-energy weekend.

Whether you’re a beginner or experienced, you’ll find a supportive environment to learn fast and ship.

Themes we explore

Themes evolve every year, but the mission stays the same: sustainability-first technology.
