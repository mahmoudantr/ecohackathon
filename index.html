Who is EcoHack for?

EcoHack welcomes university students and early-career builders interested in sustainability. Beginners and experienced participants can learn and build together. Check the next edition’s announcement for its eligibility details.

Can I come without a team?

The existing EcoHack format includes team formation at the start, so you can meet other builders. Follow the next edition’s application details for team requirements.

What will I work on?

EcoHack explores themes including waste, cities, renewable energy, agriculture, water, circular economy, climate education, and resilience. Specific challenges are announced for each edition.

When and where is the next edition?

The next edition’s dates, venue, and application timeline have not been announced on this site. Register your interest on this website to share your details with the organizing team.

Is there a participation fee?

A fee policy for the next edition has not been confirmed here. Please check its announcement or contact the team before making plans.

How do I get updates or get involved?

Register your interest on our website to share your details with the organizing team. Prospective mentors, judges, sponsors, and collaborators can contact the team.
