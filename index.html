FAQ

Quick answers. If you still have questions, reach out.

Who can participate?
EcoHack is open to university students and early-career builders who care about sustainability.
Do I need a team?
You can come solo—team formation happens at the start.
Is it free?
Yes. Sponsors make it possible to keep EcoHack accessible.
What if I’m a beginner?
Totally welcome. We run workshops and mentor office hours designed to get beginners shipping.
